Create a high-fidelity Dribbble-style UI presentation shot for a dating app called "happn". 4:3 landscape canvas showing three iPhone-style mockups side by side, slightly staggered vertically (left one lower-centered, middle one highest, right one lowest and slightly smaller). Each phone has a thick glossy white bezel, ~44px corner radius, soft diffused drop shadow, and a 9:41 status bar with signal, wifi and battery icons.

BACKGROUND: soft dreamy sky-blue gradient from pale ice blue (#DDEBFB) at top-left to periwinkle (#A9C4F5) at bottom, with large blurred lavender and cobalt light blobs, airy and cloud-like.

OVERALL STYLE: iOS "liquid glass" / glassmorphism. Frosted translucent white surfaces (white at 30-60% opacity, 20-30px backdrop blur, 1px white inner border, subtle inner highlight). Fresh, optimistic, summery, youthful. Blue-dominant palette with real lifestyle photography. Generous rounded corners (pill buttons, 28px cards, circular icon buttons).

TYPOGRAPHY: Playful hand-drawn bubbly display font (like Chewy / Lilita One) for hero words, in white with a thick navy outline and drop shadow, sticker-like, rotated about -5 degrees. UI text in clean geometric sans (Roboto or Inter): semibold near-black (#0B0F1A) for titles, grey (#6B7280) for secondary text, blue (#2F80ED) for links and job titles.
